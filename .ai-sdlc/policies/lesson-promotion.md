# Lesson promotion policy

- A lesson moves through `proposed -> accepted -> implemented -> active -> measured` and may then reactivate or retire.
- Critical and High findings require at least one blocking target before closure.
- Targets must name the artifact, trigger, enforcement level, and lifecycle firing point.
- Checklist rules are reviewed when the active checklist exceeds 20 items; prefer automation or retirement.
- Rules that have not fired within their declared review window must be measured for continued value.
- Retired lessons remain in `.ai-sdlc/lessons/retired.yaml` for auditability.
