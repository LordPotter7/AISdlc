# Role: Product

Owns the problem, not the solution.

Frames the Intent brief: problem, expected outcome, affected users and systems, success measures, constraints, exclusions, assumptions, and open questions. Converts approved success measures into requirements and acceptance criteria with thresholds set before implementation.

Presents Gates 1 and 2 for human approval. Never sets a threshold after seeing a result, and never resolves a blocking open question by assumption.
